# Salisbury Steak Dinner

**Prep Time:** 20 min | **Cook Time:** 40 min | **Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

### Salisbury Steaks

- 1.5 lbs 80/20 ground beef
- 1/3 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

### Mushroom Gravy

- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 1 medium yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Mashed Potatoes

- 2 lbs Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or whole milk, warmed
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Corn

- 1 cup corn kernels (frozen or fresh)
- 1 tbsp butter
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Green Goddess Salad

- 1 large head romaine or butter lettuce, chopped
- 1 ripe avocado
- 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves
- 2 tbsp fresh tarragon or parsley
- 2 tbsp chives, chopped
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup buttermilk or whole milk
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 clove garlic
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

### Green Goddess Dressing (Make First)

1. Blend avocado, basil, tarragon, chives, sour cream, buttermilk, mayonnaise, lemon juice, and garlic in a blender until smooth.
2. Season with salt and pepper. Thin with extra buttermilk if needed. Refrigerate until ready to serve.

### Mashed Potatoes

3. Cover potatoes with cold salted water in a large pot. Bring to a boil and cook 15–18 min until fork-tender.
4. Drain well. Return to pot over low heat for 1 min to steam off excess moisture.
5. Mash with butter, then stir in warmed cream until smooth. Season with salt and pepper. Keep warm.

### Salisbury Steaks

6. Combine all steak ingredients in a bowl. Mix until just combined — don't overwork.
7. Form into 4 oval patties, about 3/4 inch thick.
8. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear patties 3–4 min per side until browned. Transfer to a plate — they will finish in the gravy.

### Mushroom Gravy

9. Reduce heat to medium. Add butter to the same skillet. Sauté onions 5 min until softened.
10. Add mushrooms and cook 4–5 min until browned. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
11. Sprinkle flour over vegetables and stir to coat. Cook 1 min.
12. Pour in beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, and thyme. Scrape up any browned bits. Bring to a simmer.
13. Return patties to the skillet. Simmer uncovered 10–12 min, turning once, until cooked through and gravy has thickened. Season to taste.

### Corn

14. While steaks simmer, melt butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add corn, cook 3–4 min until heated through. Season with salt and pepper.

### Salad

15. Toss chopped lettuce with green goddess dressing just before serving.

## Notes

- Make the dressing up to 2 days ahead — it keeps well and the flavors deepen.
- Yukon Golds make the creamiest mash; don't substitute russets unless that's all you have.
- Don't crowd the patties when searing — work in batches if your skillet is small.
- The gravy will continue to thicken as it sits; thin with a splash of broth if needed.
- Leftover steaks reheat well in a covered pan with a little broth over low heat.
